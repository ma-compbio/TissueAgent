### H1

**Statement (narrowed):** Fibroblasts at lymphatic–epicardial interface regions, defined as adventitial fibroblast (adFibro) cells whose nearest spatial neighbors include lymphatic endothelial cells (LEC) or epicardial-derived cells (EPDC), exhibit a distinct, multi-gene extracellular-matrix and stromal-interaction expression program compared with adFibro cells lacking such neighbors and with other fibroblast populations.

**Grounded in:** OBSERVATION 2, OBSERVATION 3, OBSERVATION 4

**Decision:** KEEP

**Evidence summary:** H1 supported: interface adFibro (n=798) vs non-interface adFibro (n=764) showed 30 genes significantly upregulated in the interface group at FDR<0.05 and log2FC>0.25. The top interface-enriched genes included CLDN5, LYVE1, PENK, NKD2, HAND1. Comparison against aFibro and vFibro indicated that many of these genes are also higher in interface adFibro than in other fibroblast types, consistent with a distinct interface-associated stromal program.
