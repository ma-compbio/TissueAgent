# Limited Background — Kidney cell-state atlas (Lake et al., 2023)

**Tissue:** Human kidney (healthy and injured / disease contexts).

**Technology:** Multi-omic atlas including single-nucleus / single-cell RNA-seq
and spatial transcriptomics (Visium / Slide-seq) with accompanying imaging.

**Annotations available:** cell-type and cell-state labels spanning epithelial
tubule subclasses, endothelial, stromal, immune, and neural populations.
Spatial coordinates (when present) are in `.obsm["spatial"]`.

**Scope of study (intentionally vague):** the dataset profiles healthy and
altered cell states and their spatial niches in the human kidney.
**Specific findings — including which injured / adaptive / degenerative states
the authors defined, which immune–epithelial niches they highlighted, and which
repair signatures they linked to function decline — are withheld from you.**
Discover what is interesting by exploring the provided dataset directly.

**Constraints:**
- Use only this dataset; no external lookups.
- Do not relabel author-provided cell types / states.
- Treat spatial coordinates as unitless if scale is not provided.
